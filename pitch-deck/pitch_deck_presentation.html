The Problem

Wound Care is Trapped Behind Cost & Complexity

Millions of patients suffer from chronic wounds without access to modern therapeutic interventions.

Prohibitive Capital Cost

Incumbent NPWT machines cost thousands of dollars, pricing out secondary hospitals and nursing homes.

Bulky & Inflexible

Heavy consoles confined to metro ICUs cause logistical bottlenecks and nursing fatigue.

Avoidable Complications

Reliance on basic dressings causes delayed healing, high infection rates, and elevated amputation risks.
