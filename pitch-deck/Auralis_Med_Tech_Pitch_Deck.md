## Slide 2: Problem

### Slide Layout & Concept
* **Visual Style:** 3-column split card layout: *Prohibitive Cost*, *Infrastructure Inequity*, and *Clinical Complications*.
* **Key Headline:** *"Millions suffer from chronic wounds, yet standard-of-care NPWT remains inaccessible to the vast majority of healthcare providers."*

### Key Content
* **The Burden of Non-Healing Wounds:**  
  Chronic wounds—including Diabetic Foot Ulcers (DFUs), pressure ulcers, venous ulcers, and complex post-surgical wounds—require weeks to months of intensive care, placing a heavy burden on healthcare resources.
* **The Accessibility Divide:**  
  * **Capital Intensity:** Traditional commercial NPWT devices have high acquisition and rental costs, pricing out smaller nursing homes, community clinics, and tier-2/tier-3 hospitals.
  * **Logistical Complexity:** Many existing systems are heavy, non-intuitive, and depend on complex servicing networks.
* **The Consequence:**  
  Clinicians in lower-resource settings are forced to rely on conventional wet-to-dry dressings, leading to prolonged hospitalization, increased infection risk, higher overall treatment costs, and elevated risks of avoidable amputations.

## Slide 3: Our Solution – SAFE VAC

### Slide Layout & Concept
* **Visual Style:** Hero render of SAFE VAC highlighting compact form factor, user-friendly controls, secure canister system, and standard dressing integration.
* **Key Headline:** *"Delivering standard-compliant Negative Pressure Wound Therapy in a compact, intuitive, and highly accessible footprint."*

### Key Content
* **Consistent Therapeutic Delivery:**  
  Engineered to generate controlled, continuous, or intermittent negative pressure across wound beds within recognized clinical therapeutic ranges to support exudate removal and granulation tissue formation.
* **Ultra-Compact & Portable Architecture:**  
  Ergonomically designed for bedside flexibility, easy intra-hospital transport, and potential transition across diverse clinical care workflows.
* **Simplified Clinical Interface:**  
  Clear, intuitive controls and monitoring indicators that allow nursing staff and medical officers to operate and monitor therapy with minimal onboarding friction.
* **Safe Exudate Management:**  
  Robust canister and tubing interface featuring dependable fluid handling and leak-resistant sealing mechanics to maintain clinical hygiene and safety standards.
* **Universal Compatibility:**  
  Designed to integrate seamlessly with standard, clinically approved wound dressing kits and sealing drapes.
